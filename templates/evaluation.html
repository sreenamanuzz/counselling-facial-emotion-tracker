Grad-CAM Interpretability

In deep convolutional layers and Conformer attention heads, Grad-CAM calculates the gradient of the winning emotion class with respect to feature activation maps.

  • Brow Furrow (Corrugator AU4): Dominant in Angry and Sad
  • Smile Curvature (Zygomaticus AU12): Dominant in Happy and Romantic
  • Eye Aperture & High Frontalis: Dominant in Surprise and Rock
  • Symmetrical Resting Landmarks: Characteristic of Relax
SHAP Game Theory Attribution

SHAP leverages cooperative game theory (Shapley values) to fairly distribute the credit among individual multi-modal feature inputs (acoustic pitch, ocular aspect ratio, linguistic tokens).
