Scientific Abstract & Research Scope

Facial Expression Tracking for Video Based Counselling Engagement

Abstract

Facial emotion recognition is an important application of machine learning and computer vision, with potential uses in security systems, mental health monitoring, and human-computer interaction. This work presents a multi-modal Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) and Conformer-based approach for recognizing human emotions from facial expressions and conversational cues during video-based tele-counseling. The system utilizes the AffectNet facial expression dataset and video counseling engagement corpus containing images representing seven major emotional states: angry, happy, relax, rock, romantic, sad, and surprise. The pipeline pre-processes visual frames via grayscale normalization, resizing, facial landmark extraction, and spatial-temporal modeling, supplemented by speech acoustic analysis and multilingual transcript interpretation in Malayalam, English, and Hindi. Integrated Explainable AI (SHAP and Grad-CAM) provides transparency for clinical practitioners, supporting objective therapeutic alliance tracking and actionable counseling engagement interventions.

Clinical Purpose & Value

In tele-mental health and video-based psychotherapy, counselors often experience perceptual fatigue or miss fleeting micro-expressions. This system acts as an unintrusive clinical companion, providing objective real-time indicators of client arousal, emotional breakthroughs, or withdrawal.

Ethics & Patient Privacy

The application operates strictly under clinical consent protocols. Real-time video processing is performed in-session with no unauthorized cloud retention, and data points are mapped to evidence-based psychological recommendations (e.g. Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, somatic grounding, de-escalation).

Implemented Algorithms & Technologies

Random Forest & XGBoost

Robust tree-based ensembles trained on extracted facial action units, geometric aspect ratios (EAR, MAR), and acoustic MFCC speech features. Yields high interpretability and quick inference.

CRNN with Attention

A Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network with temporal self-attention. CNN feature extractors process video frames, while Bi-directional GRU layers capture temporal evolution and emotional shifts throughout counseling sessions.

CNN + Conformer

Combines convolution layers for localized micro-expression features with Transformer Multi-Head Self-Attention for global context, reaching a state-of-the-art 93.7% accuracy on benchmark tests.

Explainable AI: SHAP & Grad-CAM

Grad-CAM renders visual attention heatmaps over facial musculature, while SHAP computes exact Shapley attributions, ensuring practitioners know precisely why an emotion was detected.
